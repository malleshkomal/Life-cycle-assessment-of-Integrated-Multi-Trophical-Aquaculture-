# Hi, I'm Komal Mallesh Chougule 👋

**LCA researcher** – life cycle assessment, circular economy and structured life cycle inventory (LCI) data.
I turn messy material, energy and product data into reliable sustainability indicators that support decisions.

### 🔬 What I work on
- Life cycle assessment according to ISO 14040/44 (openLCA, Brightway, GaBi, Umberto; ecoinvent, Agribalyse)
- Prospective LCA of hydrogen and Power-to-Liquid fuels (premise, IMAGE, REMIND)
- Circular systems and KPI frameworks (IMTA aquaculture, nutrient recovery, waste valorisation)
- Reproducible LCI data: schemas, metadata, version control (Python, GitHub)

### 📌 Featured project
**[imta-cirkpi](../imta-cirkpi)** – a transparent circular KPI decision tool for aquaculture:
LCI in the LCIS2024 format → LCA in openLCA (ecoinvent 3.7, EF 3.1) → 12 KPIs on environment, circularity and economy
(Excel + Python), with documented assumptions and known issues.
