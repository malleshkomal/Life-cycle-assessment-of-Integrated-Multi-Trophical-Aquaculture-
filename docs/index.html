Life cycle assessment of fish monoculture and IMTA aquaculture

Circular KPI decision tool: gilthead sea bream farm in Portugal, A: fish monoculture vs C: IMTA (fish + seaweed + mussels) with valorisation of seaweed residues and mussel shells. Functional unit: 1 kg fresh sea bream at farm gate, economic allocation.

Demonstration study. Activity data are assumptions checked against literature ranges; KPI values use illustrative impact factors, not ecoinvent. ecoinvent results and their open issues are shown in section 5.

Key findings

  1. IMTA lowers the footprint per kg of fish (2.90 → 2.12 kg CO2-eq/kg) and recovers about 18 % of the nitrogen the fish release.
  2. The farm as a whole emits about 9 % more CO2 (energy, boats, ropes, transport) – an absolute-vs-relative trade-off.
  3. Waste valorisation lifts circularity KPIs (by-product valorisation 0 → 100 %) but its climate effect is small next to feed.
  4. Feed is the hotspot; in ecoinvent, land use change from Brazilian soy is a major contributor.
