How the fish waste is used in pond IMTA

One production cycle per system. Switch between the three systems of the INTEGRATE study.

  1. Feed brings 127 kg N into the pond.
  2. Oysters filter particles (uneaten feed, faeces, plankton); fish and oysters together hold 29.0 kg N.
  3. Macroalgae take up dissolved N and P: 30.6 kg N in 1,224 kg of algae.
  4. 67.2 kg N is still released – 47% of the feed N is recovered in products.
  1. Feed brings 5.7 kg N into the pond.
  2. Oysters filter particles (uneaten feed, faeces, plankton); fish and oysters together hold 4.5 kg N.
  3. Macroalgae take up dissolved N and P: 1.8 kg N in 35,400 kg of algae.
  4. The system takes up more N than the feed brings: a net uptake of 0.5 kg N from the estuary water (reported in Table 5). Fish and oysters also eat natural food.
  1. Only fish are farmed. Of the 738 kg N in the feed, 149 kg ends up in the fish.
  2. 589 kg N (80%) leaves with the outflow water – there are no oysters or macroalgae to catch it.

Data: INTEGRATE deliverable PI05#3, Tables 4 and 5 (IPMA, CTAQUA 2020). Fish and oyster N are reported together. Part of the repository “Life cycle assessment of fish monoculture and IMTA aquaculture”.
